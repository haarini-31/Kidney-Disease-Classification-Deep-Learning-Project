About RenalScan AI

AI-Assisted Kidney Imaging & Reporting System

The Problem

Medical imaging data, particularly CT scans, require immense time and expertise to accurately review. Radiologists face high workloads, which can lead to fatigue and potential diagnostic delays. There is a strong need for assistive technologies that can quickly pre-screen and highlight potential abnormalities, allowing medical professionals to prioritize and focus their attention where it's most needed.

The Solution

RenalScan AI provides a streamlined, AI-assisted workflow. By integrating deep learning directly into the clinical interface, the system processes uploaded kidney CT scans and provides an immediate initial classification. This serves as a "second set of eyes" for the radiologist, bridging the gap between raw medical imagery and final clinical reporting.

The Workflow

1
Patient Management Securely register and manage patient demographics and histories within the SQLite database.
2
Scan Upload Upload localized kidney CT imagery directly into the patient's workflow profile.
3
AI Inference The VGG16 model automatically analyzes the image, outputting a prediction (Cyst, Normal, Stone, Tumor) and a confidence probability.
4
Human Review A credentialed medical professional (Doctor/Radiologist) reviews the scan, agrees/disagrees with the AI, and adds clinical notes and recommendations.
5
Final Reporting The system merges the initial AI output and the final human review into a cohesive, printable PDF medical report.

Research & Demonstration Disclaimer

This application is an educational and research prototype. It is not a certified medical device and has not received FDA or equivalent clinical approval. The AI classifications are assistive research outputs and do not constitute a medical diagnosis. All AI-generated results must be reviewed, verified, and interpreted by a qualified healthcare professional.
